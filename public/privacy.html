Gunzalez Price Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

gunzalezserg.com is a gun and ammo price tracker run by Sergio Gonzalez (@gunzalezserg) in Dallas, Texas. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What I collect

  • Price alerts and Gun Finder requests. When you set an alert, I save your email address, what you're watching (gun, caliber, UPC or SKU), your target price, and anything optional you add, like your state or Instagram handle. You also confirm you're 21 or older.
  • Instagram messages. If you message @gunzalezserg or comment a keyword like DEALS or PRICE, my automations save your Instagram username, the message, and which automation replied, so I can answer you and keep track of the conversation.
  • Basic site data. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs (like IP address and pages requested) to run and protect the site. I don't use advertising trackers or sell any data.

How I use it

  • To email you when a deal matches your alert.
  • To reply to your messages and comments on Instagram.
  • To see which guns and calibers people want tracked, so I know what to post.

I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't add you to any list you didn't sign up for. If my Instagram account is ever taken down, I may use your email once to let you know where to find me.

Who helps run this

These services store or process data for the site: Cloudflare (hosting and the price database), Airtable (alert requests and contacts), Google Gmail (sending alert emails), n8n (the automations that connect them), and Meta / Instagram (messages). Each has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and embeds

The site itself doesn't set tracking cookies. The Videos page shows Instagram and YouTube videos; when you load those, Instagram or YouTube may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Links to stores

Deal links take you to other stores. Some are affiliate links (see the affiliate disclosure). Once you leave this site, that store's privacy policy applies.

Your choices

  • Stop an alert: click "Stop this alert" or "Unsubscribe" in any alert email.
  • Delete your info: email gunzalezserg@gmail.com and I'll remove your alerts and contact details.
  • Instagram: DM me "stop" and I'll pause the automations for you.

Age

This site is for adults. Price alerts require you to confirm you're 21 or older, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
